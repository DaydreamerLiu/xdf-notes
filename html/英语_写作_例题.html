一、语法改错题

1.1 谓语动词个数（黄金法则）

题目 1: There are many people take part in sports and games now.

解析
错误: 一个简单句出现了两个谓语动词 (are 和 take)，违反黄金法则。 改正: There are many people who take part in sports and games now. 用定语从句引导词 who 作为"门票"将第二个谓语引入句子。

题目 2: Many people who get married before they are mature enough.

解析
错误: 整个句子只有 who 从句中有谓语 (get married + are)，主句没有谓语动词，"who get married before they are mature enough" 成了 people 的定语，句子不完整。 改正: Many people get married before they are mature enough. 去掉 who，让 get married 成为主句谓语。

1.2 动词变形

题目: I advise you to visit your teacher who teach you College English.

解析
错误: 定语从句主语 who 指代 teacher（单数），谓语动词应加三单。 改正: I advise you to visit your teacher who teaches/taught you College English. 注意时态根据语境也可用 taught。

1.3 名词变形

题目 1: Employee can get more benefits from this policy than employer.

解析
错误: 可数名词 employee/employer 在泛指时应用复数形式。 改正: Employees can get more benefits from this policy than employers.

题目 2: He had many interesting experience while travelling in Africa.

解析
错误: experience 作"经历"讲时是可数名词，many 后需用复数。 改正: He had many interesting experiences while travelling in Africa.

1.4 比较级

题目: Li Hua is more kinder than I.

解析
错误: kinder 本身就是比较级，不能再加 more。这是最典型的"中式比较级"错误。 改正: Li Hua is kinder than I.

1.5 词性混淆

题目 1: Due to the rapidly development of China's economy, this phenomenon is becoming increasing prevalent.

解析
错误: rapidly 是副词，不能修饰名词 development；increasing 是形容词，不能修饰形容词 prevalent。 改正: Due to the rapid development of China's economy, this phenomenon is becoming increasingly prevalent. 记住: 形容词修饰名词，副词修饰动词/形容词/副词。

题目 2: Some people firmly agree, but others against it.

解析
错误: against 是介词，不是动词，不能直接作谓语。 改正: Some people firmly agree, but others are against it. 类似错误: "I against the plan" → "I am against the plan"。

1.6 介词误用

题目 1: Under his help, I finished the task in time.

解析
错误: 中文"在他的帮助下"用 under 是逐字硬翻。英文固定搭配是 with one's help。 改正: With his help, I finished the task in time.

题目 2: She has difficulty on learning English.

解析
错误: have difficulty 固定搭配介词是 in，不是 on。 改正: She has difficulty in learning English.

1.7 选词不当

题目 1: I opened the television to watch a funny movie.

解析
错误: open 用于物理上"打开"(开门/开窗/开书)，电器设备"打开"用 turn on。 改正: I turned on the television to watch a funny movie.

题目 2: You should eat this medicine three times a day.

解析
错误: 中文"吃药"的"吃"不能直译为 eat。英文固定搭配是 take medicine。 改正: You should take this medicine three times a day.

1.8 拼写错误

题目 1: In morden society, the goverment should take measures to protect the enviroment.

解析
改正: In modern society, the government should take measures to protect the environment.

题目 2: It is convinient to use this method to inform you of this case.

解析
改正: It is convenient to use this method to inform you of this case.

1.9 漏词

题目 1: There's something that I'd like to talk.

解析
错误: talk 是不及物动词，需要加介词 about 才能带宾语 (something)。 改正: There's something that I'd like to talk about.

题目 2: We have problem that needs to be solved immediately.

解析
错误: problem 是可数名词单数，前面需要冠词。 改正: We have a problem that needs to be solved immediately.

1.10 语序

题目: I know that you are interested in Chinese traditional cooking.

解析
错误: 形容词顺序规则：国籍(Chinese)应在性质/描述类(traditional)之前？不对--口诀"美小圆旧黄，法国木书房"中，"法国"(国籍/来源)在"旧"(新旧)之后，但 traditional 是属于"用途/类别"类还是"性质"类？ 正确顺序: 描述类 → 大小 → 形状 → 新旧 → 颜色 → 来源/国籍 → 材料 → 用途 → 中心名词 Chinese 是国籍/来源，traditional 是描述/性质。因此描述(traditional)在前，国籍(Chinese)在后。 改正: I know that you are interested in traditional Chinese cooking.

2.2 数据描述 -- 此消彼长型

题目 1 (2011 英语二): 国产品牌 25%→33%，日系品牌 35%→25%

参考表达
According to the chart, the market share of domestic brands rose from 26% to 33%.
By contrast, that of Japanese brands dropped from 35% to 25% during the same period. 或使用句式二: In detail, there was a sharp increase in the market share of domestic car brands from 25% in 2008 to about 32% in 2009.
By contrast, this period witnessed a rapid decline in the proportion of Japanese ones from 35% to 25%.

2.3 数据描述 -- 趋势相同幅度不同型

题目 1 (2010 英语二): 发展中国家 0.4b→4b，发达国家 0.6b→1b

参考表达
基础版: According to the chart, the number of users in developing countries surged from 0.4 billion to 4 billion, while the figure in developed countries increased gradually from 0.6 billion to 1 billion. 进阶版: We can see continued growth in the numbers of mobile-phone users both in developing and developed countries these years.
A closer look, however, shows a striking difference: the number of users in developing countries grew at a much faster pace, from about 0.4 billion to 4 billion, while the figure in developed countries just witnessed a steady increase, from 0.6 billion to 1 billion.

题目 2 (2017 英语二): 博物馆数量 4165→4692，参观人数 637.8 万→781.1 万

参考表达
The line chart clearly illustrates the changes in the numbers of museums and their visitors in China from 2013 to 2015.
Specifically, the number of museums increased moderately from 4165 in 2013 to 4692 in 2015.
Meanwhile, this period witnessed a sharp rise in the number of visitors from 637.8 million to 781.1 million.

题目 3 (2019 英语二): 就业 68.1%→60.7%，升学 26.3%→34%，创业 1.3%→2.6%

参考表达
完整首段: The column chart clearly illustrates the changes in the future plans of graduates at a certain university between 2013 and 2018.
To be specific, this period witnessed a moderate decline in the proportion of graduates who chose to work from 68.1% to 60.7%.
By contrast, there was a significant increase in the proportion of those opting for further study from 26.3% to 34.0%, and the percentage of graduates engaged in entrepreneurship rose gently from 1.3% to 2.6% during the same period.

四、图表作文 -- 主体段与结尾段

4.1 过渡句

题目: 写出两种"不难发现原因"的过渡句。

参考表达
句式一: It is not difficult to come up with some possible factors for the result/the change(s)/the phenomenon. 句式二: At least two primary factors, in my opinion, can account for the result/the change(s)/the phenomenon.

4.2 完整主体段

题目 1 (2012 英语二): 工作满意度 -- 为什么 41-50 岁最不满意？

参考表达
At least two primary factors, in my opinion, can explain the phenomenon.
For one thing, financial pressure makes it harder for middle-aged employees to feel satisfied with their jobs.
Since they have to take care of their elderly parents and young children, they may complain that their salaries barely cover their household expenses.
For another, workers aged 50 and above are more inclined to be content with their jobs, as they can perform their duties with greater efficiency and ease, owing to their abundant work experience and skills, combined with relatively lighter tasks assigned to them because of their seniority.

题目 2 (2011 英语二): 国产车份额上升原因

参考表达
At least two primary factors, in my opinion, can account for the changes.
For one thing, domestic vehicles boast competitive prices.
Due to government support for domestic automakers, such as tax incentives, interest-free loans and financial subsidies, the manufacturing costs of automobiles made in China are lower than those of foreign counterparts.
Correspondingly, their selling prices are lower, which makes them gain popularity among consumers.
For another, the quality of domestic automobiles improves significantly.
With the development of science and technology, domestic cars are continuously upgraded, which helps greatly improve their quality, including more accurate navigation systems and more reliable brakes, enabling consumers to have greater confidence in domestic cars.

题目 3 (2014 英语二): 城镇人口上升原因

参考表达
At least two primary factors, in my opinion, can account for the changes.
For one thing, cities have more job opportunities, which serves as a strong motivator for rural residents to migrate.
The majority of factories and enterprises are based in cities, offering not only a wider range of career options but also more competitive salaries.
For another, cities boast abundant educational resources, with more prestigious schools and highly qualified teachers to choose from.
As a result, parents who attach great importance to education move to cities.

题目 4 (2019 英语二): 毕业生去向变化原因

参考表达
At least two primary factors, in my opinion, can explain the changes.
For one thing, the highly competitive job market makes it harder for graduates to find a decent job.
As the number of college graduates continues to grow, those lacking confidence in securing a good job may choose to pursue a master's degree to boost their qualifications.
For another, it is clear that the government provides new graduates with startup-friendly policies, such as tax incentives and financial subsidies.
As a result, students are more motivated to pursue their entrepreneurial dreams.

题目 5 (2018 英语二): 消费者重视特色/服务 > 价格

参考表达
At least two primary factors, in my opinion, can explain the phenomenon.
For one thing, as living standards rise, people are less concerned about prices.
With economic prosperity, those with significantly higher incomes are now more willing to dine at upscale restaurants boasting superb service and a pleasant environment.

9.1 change in vs change of

题目: 填空选择 in 或 of

  2. What the country needs is a change ___ government.
解析与答案
