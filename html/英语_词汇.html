一、态度题 · 真题例句重点词

2004 英一 T3 · 公众对经济形势的态度

真题原文
"Consumers seem only mildly concerned, not panicked, and many say they remain optimistic about the economy's long-term prospects, even as they do some modest belt-tightening."
concerned /kənˈsɜːnd/ adj. 担心的; 关切的(be concerned about/with 关心…)
本段: mildly concerned 轻微担忧高频
panicked /ˈpænɪkt/ adj. 恐慌的; panic n./v. 恐慌(panic → panicked 过去式)
本段: not panicked 并不恐慌(态度题反证词)必考
optimistic /ˌɒptɪˈmɪstɪk/ adj. 乐观的; optimism n. 乐观主义
本段: remain optimistic 保持乐观必考
mildly /ˈmaɪldli/ adv. 轻微地; 温和地(to put it mildly 说得委婉些)
本段: 程度副词, 修饰 concerned重点
prospects /ˈprɒspekts/ n. 前景; 可能性(prospect 的复数, 常作"前途"讲)
本段: long-term prospects 长期前景高频

belt-tightening /ˈbelt taɪtnɪŋ/ n. 勒紧腰带; 缩减开支 — 本段: modest belt-tightening 适度节约重点

2012 英一 T4 · Donahue 对公共部门系统的态度

真题原文
"John Donahue at Harvard's Kennedy School points out that the norms of culture in Western civil services suit those who want to stay put but is bad for high achievers. ... Bankers' fat pay packets have attracted much criticism, but a public-sector system that does not reward high achievers may be a much bigger problem for America."
norms /nɔːmz/ n. 规范; 准则(norm 复数; social norms 社会规范)
本段: 文化规范高频

civil services n. 文职部门; 公共行政机构(civil servant 公务员) — 本段: 西方公共服务部门必考 stay put /steɪ pʊt/ 固定搭配: 待在原处; 安于现状 — 本段: 想安于现状的人重点 high achievers n. 高成就者; 表现优异者(achieve v. 达成) — 本段: 高成就者(公共部门不奖励他们=问题)必考 fat pay packets n. 丰厚的薪酬(fat 引申"丰厚的"; pay packet 工资袋) — 本段: 银行家丰厚薪酬重点

reward /rɪˈwɔːd/ v./n. 奖赏; 回报
本段: 不奖励高成就者高频

2014 英一 T4 · 作者对 AAAS 报告的态度

真题原文
"'The Heart of the Matter,' the just-released report by the American Academy of Arts and Sciences (AAAS), deserves praise for affirming the importance of the humanities and social sciences ... Regrettably, however, the report's failure to address the true nature of the crisis facing liberal education may cause more harm than good."

deserves praise v. 值得赞扬(deserve 应得, 值得; praise 表扬) — 本段: 先肯定 → 为后文转折做铺垫必考

affirming /əˈfɜːmɪŋ/ v. 肯定; 确认(affirm 现在分词; affirmation n.)
本段: 肯定人文学科的重要性重点
humanities /hjuːˈmænətiz/ n. 人文学科(复数常作学科名)
本段: 人文学科高频

Regrettably, however 然而遗憾的是(态度题转折信号词) — 本段: 转折词, 后面才是作者真实态度必考 failure to address 未能处理(fail to do sth. 未能做某事的名词化) — 本段: 报告未能处理问题本质重点 liberal education n. 通识教育; 人文教育 — 本段: 通识教育面临的危机高频 may cause more harm than good 弊大于利 — 本段: 整体负面评价(态度题关键句)必考

2017 英二 T1 · 作者对英国政府体育政策的态度

真题原文
"But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education. Instead of... wordy, worthy strategies, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive."
successive /səkˈsesɪv/ adj. 连续的; 接连的(succession n.)
本段: 历届政府高频
presided over /prɪˈzaɪd/ 主持; 掌管(preside over 常含"放任…发生"贬义)
本段: 历届政府放任了这些负面行为重点

squeezing money 挤压资金(squeeze v. 挤压, 榨取) — 本段: 从地方当局挤钱必考 declining attention 对…的关注下降(declining 下降中的, decline v./n.) — 本段: 教育中体育受关注度下降重点

wordy /ˈwɜːdi/ adj. 冗长的; 废话多的
本段: 空话连篇的策略重点
thrive /θraɪv/ v. 兴旺; 茁壮成长(throve/thrived, thriven)
本段: 让体育蓬勃发展高频

2016 英一 T3 · 作者对 Friedman 关于 CSR 观点的态度

真题原文
"'There is one and only one social responsibility of business,' wrote Milton Friedman ... 'to use its resources and engage in activities designed to increase its profits.' But even if you accept Friedman's premise ... things may not be absolutely clear-cut. New research suggests that CSR may create monetary value for companies — at least when they are prosecuted for corruption."

social responsibility n. 社会责任(CSR = corporate social responsibility) — 本段: 企业社会责任必考 engage in 从事; 参与 — 本段: 从事以盈利为目的的活动高频

premise /ˈpremɪs/ n. 前提; 假定
本段: 接受 Friedman 的前提高频

clear-cut /ˌklɪə ˈkʌt/ adj. 明确的; 清晰的 — 本段: 事情并非绝对清晰(转折后质疑)重点

monetary /ˈmʌnɪtri/ adj. 货币的; 金钱的(monetary value 货币价值)
本段: CSR 可能创造货币价值高频
prosecuted /ˈprɒsɪkjuːtɪd/ v. 起诉; 检举(prosecution n.)
本段: 被控腐败时重点

本段: 新技术的潜在影响高频

discard /dɪsˈkɑːd/ v. 抛弃; 丢弃
