例1. 公众对当前经济形势的态度 (2004 英一 Text3)

Q 32. How do the public feel about the current economic situation?
A. Optimistic.
B. Confused.
C. Carefree.
D. Panicked.
定位原文
①Even before Alan Greenspan's admission that America's red-hot economy is cooling, lots of working folks had already seen signs of the slowdown themselves. ②From car dealerships to Gap outlets, sales have been lagging for months as shoppers temper their spending. ③For retailers, who last year took in 24 percent of their revenue between Thanksgiving and Christmas, the cautious approach is coming at a crucial time. ④Already, experts say, holiday sales are off 7 percent from last year's pace. ⑤But don't sound any alarms just yet. ⑥Consumers seem only mildly concerned, not panicked, and many say they remain optimistic about the economy's long-term prospects, even as they do some modest belt-tightening.
解析
① 定位: 原文第二段最后一句 —— Consumers seem only mildly concerned, not panicked, and many say they remain optimistic about the economy's long-term prospects.
② 解题思路: 原文直接给出"remain optimistic"（保持乐观），态度词与选项 A 直接对应。注意: "mildly concerned, not panicked" 排除了 D（恐慌），而 confused 和 carefree 在原文中均未出现。
③ 排除干扰项: B. Confused — 原文未提及困惑；C. Carefree — 漠不关心；D. Panicked — 原文明确说"not panicked"。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例2. Donahue 对公共部门系统的态度 (2012 英一 Text4)

Q 40. John Donahue's attitude towards the public-sector system is one of _____.
A. disapproval
B. appreciation
C. tolerance
D. indifference
定位原文
①John Donahue at Harvard's Kennedy School points out that the norms of culture in Western civil services suit those who want to stay put but is bad for high achievers. ②The only American public-sector workers who earn well above \$\150,000 a year are university sports coaches and the president of the United States. ③Bankers' fat pay packets have attracted much criticism, but a public-sector system that does not reward high achievers may be a much bigger problem for America.
解析
① 定位: 第七段 —— 整段都是 Donahue 对公共部门制度的评价。
② 解题思路: 关键词 "bad for high achievers", "does not reward high achievers", "a much bigger problem" 均为负面态度标记。Donahue 批评该制度不奖励高成就者，态度为否定/反对。
③ 排除干扰项: B. appreciation — 与负面态度相反；C. tolerance — 容忍（弱于"不满"）；D. indifference —— 作者写文章讨论该话题，不可能 indifferent。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例3. 作者对 AAAS 报告的态度 (2014 英一 Text4)

Q 36. According to Paragraph 1, what is the author's attitude toward the AAAS's report?
A. Critical.
B. Appreciative.
C. Contemptuous.
D. Tolerant.
定位原文
①"The Heart of the Matter", the just-released report by the American Academy of Arts and Sciences (AAAS), deserves praise for affirming the importance of the humanities and social sciences to the prosperity and security of liberal democracy in America. ②Regrettably, however, the report's failure to address the true nature of the crisis facing liberal education may cause more harm than good.
解析
① 定位: 第一段。
② 解题思路: 关键逻辑链: "deserves praise"（值得赞扬）→ "Regrettably, however"（然而，遗憾的是）→ "failure" + "may cause more harm than good"（弊大于利）。先肯定后转折批评，整体态度是"批判性的"。注意: contemptuous（轻蔑的）程度过重，原文是理性批评而非轻蔑。
③ 排除干扰项: B. Appreciative — 只有前半句肯定，忽略了 however 后的主观点；C. Contemptuous — 程度过重；D. Tolerant — 容忍，原文是主动批判。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例4. 作者对英国政府体育政策的态度 (2017 英二 Text1)

Q 25. The author's attitude to what UK governments have done for sports is _____.
A. tolerant
B. critical
C. uncertain
D. sympathetic
定位原文
①Indeed, there is something a little absurd in the state getting involved in the planning of such a fundamentally "grassroots" concept as community sports associations. ②If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods — making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools. ③But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education. ④Instead of wordy, worthy strategies, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive. ⑤Or at least not make them worse.
解析
① 定位: 第四段 —— "But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education."
② 解题思路: "selling green spaces"（出售绿地）、"squeezing money"（挤出资金）、"declining attention"（减少关注）——一连串负面动作。"Instead of...need to do more"（不应......而应......）是典型的批评性建议句。态度为 critical。
③ 排除干扰项: A. tolerant — 容忍；C. uncertain — 不确定；D. sympathetic — 同情。均与原文的批判性措辞不符。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例5. 作者对米尔顿·弗里德曼关于 CSR 观点的态度 (2016 英一 Text3)

Q 31. The author views Milton Friedman's statement about CSR with _____.
A. tolerance
B. skepticism
C. uncertainty
D. approval
定位原文
①"There is one and only one social responsibility of business", wrote Milton Friedman, a Nobel Prize-winning economist, "That is, to use its resources and engage in activities designed to increase its profits." But even if you accept Friedman's premise and regard corporate social responsibility (CSR) policies as a waste of shareholders' money, things may not be absolutely clear-cut. ②New research suggests that CSR may create monetary value for companies — at least when they are prosecuted for corruption.
解析
① 定位: 第一段。
② 解题思路: 先引 Friedman 观点（企业唯一的社会责任是增加利润），然后"But even if you accept Friedman's premise... things may not be absolutely clear-cut"表明作者不完全认同。紧接着"New research suggests CSR may create monetary value"用新研究质疑 Friedman。这种"先引再质疑"的结构 = 怀疑态度(skepticism)。
③ 排除干扰项: A. tolerance — 容忍（不够主动）；C. uncertainty — 不确定（作者有明确立场倾向）；D. approval — 赞同（与 but 转折矛盾）。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例6. 作者对卡利福尼亚观点的态度 (2015 英一 Text2)

Q 27. The author's attitude toward California's argument is one of _____.
A. disapproval
B. indifference
C. tolerance
D. cautiousness
定位原文
①California has asked the justices to refrain from a sweeping ruling particularly one that upsets the old assumptions that authorities may search through the possessions of suspects at the time of their arrest. ②It is hard, the state argues, for judges to assess the implications of new and rapidly changing technologies.
解析
① 定位: 第二段提出 California 的论点，第三段和第四段为作者回应。
② 解题思路: "recklessly modest"（鲁莽的谦虚）、"lame argument"（蹩脚的论点）、"should start by discarding"（应首先抛弃）——层层递进的批评，态度是明确的 disapproval。
③ 排除干扰项: B. indifference — 作者明显有态度；C. tolerance — 不是容忍是反对；D. cautiousness — 作者态度明确而非谨慎。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例7. 作者对 Brendon Lynch 博客言论的态度 (2013 英一 Text2)

Q 30. The author's attitude towards what Brendon Lynch said in his blog is one of _____.
A. indulgence
B. understanding
C. appreciation
D. skepticism
定位原文
①Also unclear is why Microsoft has gone it alone. ②After all, it has an ad business too, which it says will comply with DNT requests, though it is still working out how. ③If it is trying to upset Google, which relies almost wholly on advertising, it has chosen an indirect method: There is no guarantee that DNT by default will become the norm. ④DNT does not seem an obviously huge selling point for Windows 8—though the firm has compared some of its other products favorably with Google's on that count before. ⑤Brendon Lynch, Microsoft's chief privacy officer, blogged: "We believe consumers should have more control." Could it really be that simple?
解析
① 定位: 第七段最后 —— Lynch 说"消费者应拥有更多控制权"，作者反问"Could it really be that simple?"（真的就这么简单？）
② 解题思路: 反问句"Could it really be that simple?"直接表达质疑——这是态度题中"问号=怀疑"的经典范例。skepticism 对应该语气。
③ 排除干扰项: A. indulgence — 纵容；B. understanding — 理解（积极态度，与反问矛盾）；C. appreciation — 欣赏（与反问矛盾）。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例8. 作者对逆转高速趋势的态度 (2013 英二 Text3)

Q 35. The author's attitude toward reversing the high-speed trend is _____.
A. tolerant
B. uncertain
C. optimistic
D. doubtful
定位原文
①Our ability to mute our hard-wired reactions by pausing is what differentiates us from animals: dogs can think about the future only intermittently or for a few minutes. ②But historically we have spent about 12 percent of our days contemplating the longer term. ③Although technology might change the way we react, it hasn't changed our nature. ④We still have the imaginative capacity to rise above temptation and reverse the high-speed trend.
解析
① 定位: 第六段 —— "We still have the imaginative capacity to rise above temptation and reverse the high-speed trend."
② 解题思路: "We still have the imaginative capacity to..." 是非常肯定的语气，表明作者相信人类有能力逆转趋势。optimistic 对应此积极判断。
③ 排除干扰项: A. tolerant — 容忍（不主动）；C. uncertain — 不确定（原文态度明确）；D. doubtful — 怀疑（与肯定语气矛盾）。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例9. 作者对 Gilbert 复兴交响乐团的态度 (2011 英一 Text1)

Q 25. Regarding Gilbert's role in revitalizing the Philharmonic, the author feels _____.
A. doubtful
B. enthusiastic
C. confident
D. puzzled
定位原文
①One possible response is for classical performers to program attractive new music that is not yet available on record. ②Gilbert's own interest in new music has been widely noted: Alex Ross, a classical-music critic, has described him as a man who is capable of turning the Philharmonic into "a markedly different, more vibrant organization." But what will be the nature of that difference? ③Merely expanding the orchestra's repertoire will not be enough. ④If Gilbert and the Philharmonic are to succeed, they must first change the relationship between America's oldest orchestra and the new audience it hopes to attract.
解析
① 定位: 最后一段 —— "But what will be the nature of that difference? Merely expanding the orchestra's repertoire will not be enough."
② 解题思路: 两个关键信号: ①反问句"What will be the nature of that difference?" + ②否定判断"Merely expanding...will not be enough" + ③条件句"If...are to succeed, they must..." 表达一种"目前做得不够、未来有不确定性"的怀疑态度 doubt。
③ 排除干扰项: B. enthusiastic — 太积极；C. confident — 缺乏信心表达；D. puzzled — 困惑（作者有判断而非困惑）。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例10. 作者对英国粮食自给自足的态度 (2021 英二 Text2)

Q 30. The author's attitude to food self-sufficiency in the UK is _____.
A. defensive
B. doubtful
C. tolerant
D. optimistic
定位原文
①Brexit is seen by some as an opportunity to reverse a recent trend towards the UK importing food. ②The country produces only about 60 percent of the food it eats, down from almost three-quarters in the late 1980s. ③A move back to self-sufficiency, the argument goes, would boost the farming industry, political sovereignty and even the nation's health. ④Sounds great ——- but how feasible is this vision?
解析
① 定位: 第二段 —— "Sounds great — but how feasible is this vision?"
② 解题思路: 先肯定"听起来不错"，然后用 but + 反问质疑可行性。与前面多个例题一样，"反问句 = 怀疑/质疑"是固定考点。
③ 排除干扰项: A. defensive — 防御性；C. tolerant — 容忍；D. optimistic — 乐观（与反问矛盾）。

【来源: 阅读课件0710】

例1. EgyptAir 空难例子的论证目的 (2017 英一 Text1)

Q 21. The crash of EgyptAir Flight 804 is mentioned to _____.

[A] explain American's tolerance of current security checks [B] stress the urgency to strengthen security worldwide [C] highlight the necessity of upgrading major U.S. airports [D] emphasize the importance of privacy protection

定位原文
①Americans are willing to tolerate time-consuming security procedures in return for increased safety. ②The crash of Egypt Air Flight 804, which terrorists may have downed over the Mediterranean Sea, provides another tragic reminder of why. ③But demanding too much of air travelers or providing too little security in return undermines public support for the process. ④And it should: Wasted time is a drag on Americans' economic and private lives, not to mention infuriating.
解析
① 定位: 第二段 —— 例子位于第二句，观点在前一句。
② 解题思路: 例子出现在中间段，向前找观点。前一句: "Americans are willing to tolerate time-consuming security procedures in return for increased safety." = 美国人情愿容忍耗时安检以换取安全。EgyptAir 空难被用作"tragic reminder of why"即解释为什么美国人容忍安检。对应 A: explain American's tolerance of current security checks。
③ 排除干扰项: B — "全球"范围扩大；C — "升级主要美国机场"原文未提；D — "隐私保护"是无关概念。

【来源: 阅读课件0711】

例2. 自行车链条例子的论证目的 (2018 英二 Text1)

Q 21. A broken bike chain is mentioned to show student's lack of _____.
A. academic training
B. practical ability
C. pioneering spirit
D. mechanical memorization
定位原文
①Mr. Koziatek is part of something pioneering. ②He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical. ③When did it become accepted wisdom that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain?
解析
① 定位: 第二段 —— "learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical." 这是观点 (观点在前)，自行车链条是例子。
② 解题思路: 观点: 学习应该是 practical 而不是 mechanical memorization。broken bike chain 作为例子说明学生缺乏实践能力 (practical ability)，对应 B。
③ 排除干扰项: A — "学术训练"不是焦点；C — "开创精神"原文未涉及；D — "机械记忆"是文章批评的对象，不是学生的缺陷。

【来源: 阅读课件0711】

例3. Orin Kerr 比较的引用目的 (2015 英一 Text2)

Q 30. Orin Kerr's comparison is quoted to indicate that _____.

篇章4. 2003 英语一 Text 4 · 医疗与死亡（"死亡是正常的"）

真题原文
第一段 ①It is said that in England death is pressing, in Canada inevitable and in California optional. ②Small wonder. ③Americans' life expectancy has nearly doubled over the past century. ④Failing hips can be replaced, clinical depression controlled, cataracts removed in a 30-minute surgical procedure. ⑤Such advances offer the aging population a quality of life that was unimaginable when I entered medicine 50 years ago. ⑥But not even a great health-care system can cure death—and our failure to confront that reality now threatens this greatness of ours.

原文: 第二段 ①Death is normal; we are genetically programmed to disintegrate and perish, even under ideal conditions. ②We all understand that at some level, yet as medical consumers we treat death as a problem to be solved. ③Shielded by third-party payers from the cost of our care, we demand everything that can possibly be done for us, even if it's useless. ④The most obvious example is late-stage cancer care. ⑤Physicians—frustrated by their inability to cure the disease and fearing loss of hope in the patient—too often offer aggressive treatment far beyond what is scientifically justified.

原文: 第三段 ①In 1950, the US spent \$12.7 billion on health care. ②In 2002, the cost will be \$1540 billion. ③Anyone can see this trend is unsustainable. ④Yet few seem willing to try to reverse it. ⑤Some scholars conclude that a government with finite resources should simply stop paying for medical care that sustains life beyond a certain age—say 83 or so. ⑥Former Colorado governor Richard Lamm has been quoted as saying that the old and infirm "have a duty to die and get out of the way", so that younger, healthier people can realize their potential.

原文: 第四段 ①I would not go that far. ②Energetic people now routinely work through their 60s and beyond, and remain dazzlingly productive. ③At 78, Viacom chairman Sumner Redstone jokingly claims to be 53. ④Supreme Court Justice Sandra Day O'Connor is in her 70s, and former surgeon general C. Everett Koop chairs an Internet start-up in his 80s. ⑤These leaders are living proof that prevention works and that we can manage the health problems that come naturally with age. ⑥As a mere 68-year-old, I wish to age as productively as they have.

原文: 第五段 ①Yet there are limits to what a society can spend in this pursuit. ②As a physician, I know the most costly and dramatic measures may be ineffective and painful. ③I also know that people in Japan and Sweden, countries that spend far less on medical care, have achieved longer, healthier lives than we have. ④As a nation, we may be over-funding the quest for unlikely cures while under-funding research on humbler therapies that could improve people's lives.

文章结构: P1: 引出矛盾——医疗技术大跃进 vs 无法治愈死亡 → P2: 死亡是正常的，但我们把它当问题去解决（晚期癌症过度治疗的例子）→ P3: 医疗开支失控（数字冲击），有人主张"超过某年龄不再支付"（Lamm 的极端言论）→ P4: 作者回应——不会走那么远，高龄者依然活力四射（保留性赞同）→ P5: 但开支确有限度——日本瑞典花得少反而活得好，我们过度追逐渺茫的疗法。

篇章5. 2012 英语二 Text 2 · 粉色与女孩身份（Pretty in pink）

真题原文
第一段 ①Pretty in pink: adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the color, yet it is pervasive in our young girls' lives. ②It is not that pink is intrinsically bad, but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance. ③Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence. ④Looking around, I despaired at the singular lack of imagination about girls' lives and interests.

原文: 第二段 ①Girls' attraction to pink may seem unavoidable, somehow encoded in their DNA, but according to Jo Paoletti, an associate professor of American Studies, it is not. ②Children were not color-coded at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them. ③What's more, both boys and girls wore what were thought of as gender-neutral dresses. ④When nursery colors were introduced, pink was actually considered the more masculine color, a pastel version of red, which was associated with strength. ⑤Blue, with its intimations of the Virgin Mary, constancy and faithfulness, symbolized femininity. ⑥It was not until the mid-1980s, when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.

原文: 第三段 ①I had not realised how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development. ②Take the toddler. ③I assumed that phase was something experts developed after years of research into children's behaviour: wrong. ④Turns out, according to Daniel Cook, a historian of childhood consumerism, it was popularised as a marketing trick by clothing manufacturers in the 1930s.

原文: 第四段 ①Trade publications counseled department stores that, in order to increase sales, they should create a "third stepping stone" between infant wear and older kids' clothes. ②It was only after "toddler" became a common shoppers' term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage. ③Splitting kids, or adults, into ever-tinier categories has proved a sure-fire way to boost profits. ④And one of the easiest ways to segment a market is to magnify gender differences – or invent them where they did not previously exist.

文章结构: P1: 现象 + 态度（粉色只是彩虹一小片，却不断把女孩身份绑在外表上；作者绝望于女孩生活想象力的匮乏）→ P2: 反驳 DNA 论——20 世纪初前根本无颜色编码（都穿白衣服），粉色一度是男孩色（红=力量的淡版），蓝色才是女孩色（圣母玛利亚/忠诚）→ P3: 营销趋势塑造我们对"孩子天性"的认知——连 toddler 阶段都是 1930s 服装商发明的营销把戏 → P4: 细分市场是稳赚买卖，性别差异被放大甚至凭空发明。全文主旨: 颜色（和"天性"）是市场制造的。

真题原文
第一段 To combat the trap of putting a premium on being busy, Cal Newport, author of Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World, recommends building a habit of "deep work"—the ability to focus without distraction.

原文: 第二段 There are a number of approaches to mastering the art of deep work—be it lengthy retreats dedicated to a specific task; developing a daily ritual; or taking a "journalistic" approach to seizing moments of deep work when you can throughout the day. Whichever approach, the key is to determine your length of focus time and stick to it.

原文: 第三段 Newport also recommends "deep scheduling" to combat constant interruptions and get more done in less time. "At any given point, I should have deep work scheduled for roughly the next month. Once on the calendar I protect this time like I would a doctor's appointment or important meeting", he writes.

原文: 第四段 ①Another approach to getting more done in less time is to rethink how you prioritize your day—in particular how we craft our to-do lists. ②Tim Harford, author of Messy: The Power of Disorder to Transform Our Lives, points to a study in the early 1980s that divided undergraduates into two groups: some were advised to set out monthly goals and study activities; others were told to plan activities and goals in much more detail, day by day.

原文: 第五段 ①While the researchers assumed that the well-structured daily plans would be most effective when it came to the execution of tasks, they were wrong: the detailed daily plans demotivated students. ②Harford argues that inevitable distractions often render the daily to-do list ineffective, while leaving room for improvisation in such a list can reap the best results.

原文: 第六段 In order to make the most of our focus and energy, we also need to embrace downtime, or as Newport suggests, "be lazy."

原文: 第七段 "Idleness is not just a vacation, an indulgence or a vice; it is as indispensable to the brain as Vitamin D is to the body... [idleness] is, paradoxically, necessary to getting any work done," he argues.

原文: 第八段 Srini Pillay, an assistant professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School, believes this counter-intuitive link between downtime and productivity may be due to the way our brains operate. When our brains switch between being focused and unfocused on a task, they tend to be more efficient.

原文: 第九段 "What people don't realise is that in order to complete these tasks they need to use both the focus and unfocus circuits in their brain", says Pillay.

B. daily schedules are indispensable to studying

引言 Act Your Shoe Size, Not Your Age. As adults, it seems that we're constantly pursuing happiness, often with mixed results. Instead, they look after their wellbeing instinctively and usually more effectively than we do as grownups. Perhaps it's time to learn a few lessons from them.

原文: 41 段 What does a child do when he's sad? He cries. When he's angry? He shouts. Scared? Probably a bit of both. But too often we take this process too far and end up suppressing emotions, especially negative ones. That's about as effective as brushing dirt under a carpet and can even make us ill. What we need to do is find a way to acknowledge and express what we feel appropriately and then—again, like children—move on.

原文: 42 段 A couple of Christmases ago, my youngest stepdaughter, who was 9 years old at the time, got a Superman T-shirt for Christmas. It cost less than a fiver but she was overjoyed, and couldn't stop talking about it. Too often we believe that a new job, bigger house or better car will be the magic silver bullet that will allow us to finally be content, but the reality is these things have little lasting impact on our happiness levels. Instead, being grateful for small things every day is a much better way to improve wellbeing.

原文: 43 段 Have you ever noticed how much children laugh? If we adults could indulge in a bit of silliness and giggling, we would reduce the stress hormones in our bodies, increase good hormones like endorphins, improve blood flow to our hearts and even have a greater chance of fighting off infection. All of which would, of course, have a positive effect on our happiness levels.

原文: 44 段 The problem with being a grownup is that there's an awful lot of serious stuff to deal with—work, mortgage payments, figuring out what to cook for dinner. Those things might be social, sporting, creative or completely random (dancing around the living room, anyone?)—it doesn't matter, so long as they're enjoyable, and not likely to have negative side effects, such as drinking too much alcohol or going on a wild spending spree if you're on a tight budget.

原文: 45 段 Having said all of the above, it's important to add that we shouldn't try too hard to be happy. Scientists tell us this can backfire and actually have a negative impact on our wellbeing. 各段首尾句:

[A] Some archaeological sites have always been easily observable — for example, the Parthenon in Athens, Greece; the pyramids of Giza in Egypt; and the megaliths of Stonehenge in southern England. ... Most archaeological sites have been known only through looters' activities. [B] In another case, American archaeologists René Millon and George Cowgill spent years systematically mapping the entire city of Teotihuacan in the Valley of Mexico... [C] How do archaeologists know where to find what they are looking for when there is nothing visible on the surface of the ground? Typically, they survey and sample (make test excavations on) large areas of terrain to determine where excavation will yield useful information. ... [D] Surveys can cover a single large settlement or entire landscapes. In one case, many researchers working around the ancient Maya city of Copán, Honduras, have located hundreds of small rural villages and individual dwellings by using aerial photographs... [E] To find their sites, archaeologists today rely heavily on systematic survey methods and a variety of high-technology tools and techniques. Airborne technologies, such as different types of radar and photographic equipment carried by airplanes or spacecraft, allow archaeologists to learn about what lies beneath the ground without digging. ... [F] Most archaeological sites, however, are discovered by archaeologists who have set out to look for them. Such searches can take years. British archaeologist Howard Carter knew that the tomb of the Egyptian pharaoh Tutankhamun existed from information found in other sites. ... [G] Ground surveys allow archaeologists to pinpoint the places where digs will be successful. Most ground surveys involve a lot of walking, looking for surface clues such as small fragments of pottery. They often include a certain amount of digging to test for buried materials...

答案: 41 C → A → 42 F → E → 43 G → 44 D → 45 B（对应思路）:

  • 41 → C（首段排除法）: C 提出问题 "How do archaeologists know where to find what they are looking for when there is nothing visible on the surface?"——全文总起。其余各段都不能做首段: A 有 these sites（指代）、B 有 In another case（另一案例）、D 有 In one case（一个案例）、E 有 their sites（指代）、F 有 however（转折）、G 有 allow archaeologists（承接"考古学家"这个已知概念）。首段 2 分必须拿。
  • 42 → F（信号词+复现）: 空前 A 尾 "Most archaeological sites have been known only through looters' activities"（大多数遗址靠盗掘者发现）; F 首 "Most archaeological sites, however, are discovered by archaeologists who have set out to look for them"——Most...sites 复现 + however 转折（不是靠盗掘者, 而是考古学家特意去找）。A→F 紧密衔接。
  • 43 → G（内容衔接）: 空前 E 讲"空中技术（airborne technologies）——不用挖就能看到地下"; G 讲"地面勘探（ground surveys）——走路找地表线索、挖坑测试"——从天上转到地面, 勘探方法的两层。G 尾 "test for buried materials" 与 D 首 "Surveys can cover..." 衔接。
  • 44 → D → 45 → B（成对案例）: D 的 "In one case, many researchers...Copán...by using aerial photographs"; B 的 "In another case, American archaeologists...Teotihuacan"——in one case / in another case 成对, D 必然紧跟 B 前（one → another）。而且都是"survey 覆盖"的实例（D 单一大聚落/整个景观, B 整个城市的系统绘图）。
💡 课堂讲解要点（8月6日录音转写, 个别词听写不清已按语境还原） • 这篇排序"线索给得多, 每个空基本二选一", 但要么 10 分要么 4 分——第三题（43）一旦做错后面全崩; 最大线索是 D 和 B 两段成对（in one case / in another case）, 第二重要的是首段不能错 • 首段判断: 排除法——代词/限定词（this/these/its/their）、转折词（however）、"另一个案例"（in another case）都不能做首段 → 只剩 C（问句总起） • 42 题: A 尾 "Most archaeological sites have been known only through looters' activities" + F 首 "Most archaeological sites, however, are discovered by archaeologists who have set out to look for them"——most...sites 复现 + however 转折, 铁打的衔接 • 43 题: E 讲空中勘探（airborne, 不挖地下就知道地下有什么）, G 讲地面勘探（ground surveys, 走路找碎片、挖坑测试）——天上到地面 • 44/45: in one case（D）→ in another case（B）成对——"这道题还考察了词汇: 真做错了, 丢分丢在词汇里"（in another case 不认识就完了） • 老师点评: 排序题看着难, 但线索给足时每个空都是二选一; 先做首段（必拿 2 分）再做确定的两连段

例文11. 排序 · 2018 英语一 Part B · 美国 EEOB 大楼的前世今生

排序中难度最高的一篇（18 年起提升难度）: 衔接词给得极少, 几乎全靠动作的时间顺序。已知位置: 41 → C → 42 → 43 → F → 44 → 45。

各段首尾句:

[A] In December of 1869, Congress appointed a commission to select a site and prepare plans and cost estimates for a new State Department Building. ... [B] Completed in 1875, the State Department's south wing was the first to be occupied, with its elegant fourstory library (completed in 1876), Diplomatic Reception Room, and other official rooms. ... [C] The State, War, and Navy Building, as it was originally known, housed the three Executive Branch Departments most intimately associated with formulating and conducting the nation's foreign policy... [D] Many of the most celebrated national figures have participated in historical events that have taken place within the EEOB's granite walls. Theodore and Franklin D. Roosevelt, William Howard Taft, Dwight D. Eisenhower... all served here... [E] The Eisenhower Executive Office Building (EEOB) commands a unique position in both the national history and the architectural heritage of the United States. ... [F] Construction took 17 years as the building slowly rose wing by wing. When the EEOB was finished, it was the largest office building in Washington, with nearly 2 miles of black and white tiled corridors... [G] The history of the EEOB began long before its foundations were laid. The first executive offices were constructed between 1799 and 1820. A series of fires... destroyed much of the original building...

答案: 41 E → C → 42 G → 43 A → F → 44 B → 45 D（对应思路）:

  • 41 → E（总起+全称）: E 用全称 The Eisenhower Executive Office Building (EEOB) 并给出定位（在国家和建筑史上地位独特）——专有名词第一次出现用全称, 只能放最前; 且 E 无任何指代。
  • 42 → G（时间倒叙）: C 讲"这座楼容纳了三个部门"; G "The history of the EEOB began long before its foundations were laid"——倒回到建楼之前的历史（"很久以前就开始"= 往最前面放, 放在 A 准备建之前）。时间线: G（楼之前的历史）→ A（1869 准备建）→ F（建了 17 年）→ B（1875 完工入住）。
  • 43 → A（时间顺序）: "In December of 1869, Congress appointed a commission to select a site and prepare plans"——准备建楼。动作时间顺序: 先有 G 的历史（1799-1820 的办公楼、火灾）, 再 A 准备（1869）, 再 F 建造（took 17 years）, 再 B 完工（Completed in 1875）。
  • 44 → B（时间顺序）: "Completed in 1875, the State Department's south wing was the first to be occupied"——建完入住, 紧跟 F（Construction took 17 years...When the EEOB was finished）之后。B 的 completed 和 F 的 finished/was the largest 呼应。
  • 45 → D（结尾）: "Many of the most celebrated national figures have participated in historical events that have taken place within the EEOB's granite walls"——楼建成后发生的历史事件（名人轶事作结）。注意 D 用 EEOB 缩写（E 是全称, D 是缩写 → D 必须在 E 之后）。
💡 课堂讲解要点（8月6日录音转写, 个别词听写不清已按语境还原） • 这篇是 18 年真题, 排序里最难的: 找不到明确的衔接词（没有那种一锤定音的线索词）, 只能靠动作的时间顺序——"这个考点是普遍的, 17 年考的还是这件事, 只是文章简单" • 时间线梳理（老师板书）: G（history began long before foundations——楼之前的历史）→ A（1869 国会任命委员会, 准备建）→ F（construction took 17 years, 建了 17 年）→ B（completed in 1875, 建完入住）——"准备建、建 17 年、建完, 动作时间顺序" • 数字别纠结: 老师说看到 "17 years" 别去算"到底是 16 年半还是 17 年"——"考的是英语不是数学, 他写 17 就 17"; 每段的大写人名（罗斯福/塔夫脱/艾森豪威尔）不认识不影响, 只当"这是一堆名人" • 全称/缩写线索: E 段第一次出现 EEOB 全称（The Eisenhower Executive Office Building）, D 段用缩写 EEOB——"全称第一次出现才能全称, 后面才能缩写; 你把缩写段排在 EOB 全称段前面就是纯思路不清晰"; "意乱"（EEOB）出来有点感觉, 肯定要往前放 • 18 年考点没变: 还是动作时间顺序 + 首段排除法; 做完觉得文章读不通顺很正常——真题原文是改写+删减过的, 别自己乱想

例文12. 排序 · 2010 英语一 Part B · 欧洲食品零售

排序里唯一"多给一段"的年份（C 段未用）: 只给一个已知段落 E, 41-44 连续四个空——线索最少、最难的一年。已知位置: 41 → 42 → 43 → 44 → E → 45, 共 7 段用 6 段。

各段首尾句:

[A] The first and more important is the consumer's growing preference for eating out; the consumption of food and drink in places other than homes has risen from about 32 percent of total consumption in 1995 to 35 percent in 2000 and is expected to approach 38 percent by 2005... [B] Retail sales of food and drink in Europe's largest markets are at a standstill, leaving European grocery retailers hungry for opportunities to grow. Most leading retailers have already tried e-commerce, with limited success, and expansion abroad... [C] Will such variations bring about a change in the overall structure of the food and drink market? Definitely not. The functioning of the market is based on flexible trends dominated by potential buyers... [D] All in all, this clearly seems to be a market in which big retailers could profitably apply their scale, existing infrastructure and proven skills in t[he coming decade]. [E] Despite variations in detail, wholesale markets in the countries that have been closely examined—France, Germany, Italy, and Spain—are made out of the same building blocks: demand, supply, and chains of intermediaries... but these are... two opposite trends [hmm, 以课件为准] [F] For example, wholesale food and drink sales come to \$268 billion in France, Germany, Italy, Spain, and the United Kingdom in 2000—more than 40 percent of retail sales... [G] However, none of these requirements should deter large retailers (and even some large food producers and existing wholesalers) from trying their hand, since they have already proved successful in other countries...

答案: 41 B → 42 F → 43 D → 44 G → E → 45 A（C 未用）:

  • 41 → B（首段排除法）: B 总起（欧洲最大市场食品饮料零售停滞, 零售商渴望增长机会）——无指代、无转折、无"另一个"; 其余: A 有 the first...（列举项第一, 前文必有"两个趋势"）、C 有 such variations（指代）、D 有 All in all（总结词, 只能做尾段）、F 有 For example（举例段, 前面必有被举例内容）、G 有 However + these requirements（转折+指代）。首段 2 分必拿。
  • 42 → F（唯一信号词）: 空前 B 尾 "leaving European grocery retailers hungry for opportunities to grow"（零售商渴望机会）; F 首 "For example, wholesale food and drink sales come to \$268 billion..."——for example 唯一信号词: 举例段紧跟被举例段（B 讲零售停滞渴望机会, F 举批发销售额的实例佐证"市场有多大"）。
  • 43 → D / 44 → G（难点, 三选二）: D "All in all, this clearly seems to be a market..."——总结段 + this 指代前文的市场描述; G "However, none of these requirements should deter large retailers from trying their hand"——However 转折 + these requirements 复数指代前文（前文必有"需求/条件"类复数描述）。E 已知在 44 后: E 讲批发市场的共同结构（demand, supply, and chains of intermediaries——需求、供给、中介链 = requirements 的具体化）, 所以 G（these requirements 指代 E 的内容）紧跟 E 前 → 44 = G; D 放 43 总结批发与零售的整体图景。C 未用: C 的 such variations 指代"细节差异", 只能放 E（Despite variations in detail）后——但 E 后只有 45, 45 = A, 塞不下 → C 淘汰。
  • 45 → A（空前线索）: 空前 E 尾 "two opposite trends"（两个相反趋势）; A 首 "The first and more important is the consumer's growing preference for eating out; the consumption of food and drink in places other than homes has risen..."——两个相反趋势的第一个: 外出就餐 vs 在家吃。空前意象+选项首位词。
💡 课堂讲解要点（8月6日录音转写, 个别词听写不清已按语境还原） • 这篇唯一的"多给一段"年份（C 未用）: "多一个选项 = 保底 2 分的全选同一个策略失效——万一多的那个就是废的, 全蒙可能一分没有"; 且 41-44 四个连续空, 线索最少, 超简单（难度很大） • 老师安排的教学重点: ① 首段 41 必须做对（排除法必拿 2 分）; ② 先做孤立能确定的题（45 是"孤立的", 先拿分）, 再做 43/44——"43、44 不好答, 确实比较难" • 41 题: 首段排除法——"the first 不行（前面得有另一个）, 转折不行, for example 不行, All in all 不行, 只剩 B" • 42 题: 空前 B 讲零售停滞渴望机会 → 空 F 讲批发销售额 40%——for example 举例段紧跟被举例段, "首尾一连, 例子就出来了" • 43/44 难点解法（老师演示换思路）: 做不进去时, 43/44 两个空肯定紧密相连——"换一个思路, 找 C/D/G 三个选项的首尾句之间的衔接"; C 的 variations 只能往 E（Despite variations）后面放, 但 E 后面只有 45（=A）, 塞不下 → C 淘汰; G 的 these requirements（复数）前面必有复数"需求"类描述; D 的 All in all 总结段 • 45 题: 空前 E 尾"两个相反的趋势" → A 首 "The first and more important is..."——"得看一个尾句（E 的尾）, 两个相反的趋势, 哪一段里有相反的趋势? A 段嘛——出去吃还是在外面吃、在家吃, 互为替代品（alternative）" • 老师总评: 排序题难度分布——17 年超简单, 18 年提升难度, 19 年后趋于稳定; "排序也好、小标题也好、七选五也好, 做完觉得文章不顺很正常——原文是改写删减过的, 别自己乱想"
